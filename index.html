Our Bakery

Ringblomman bageri has quickly become a staple of Jönköping’s foodie world. We ascribe this to our love for baking. Every loaf, every pie, every pastry is a handmade love letter to all the people who take the time out of their day to come and try our food.

Our Commitment

We strive to create the best plant-based eating experience possible. We feature vegan spins of all different kinds of pastries, eating green is not a compromise with us. Additionally, with a specially curated menu design, and well-trained staff, our accomodation for people with allergies is industry leading.
